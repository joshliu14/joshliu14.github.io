About

I care about the whole machine learning pipeline: the data that goes in, the model in the middle, and the latency on the way out.

I'm pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Tufts, where I'm a graduate researcher in the Datacenter Systems Lab studying how to make ML inference on CPUs faster and more efficient. I graduated from Brandeis with a B.S. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics, magna cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa, and played varsity baseball there.

I've trained computer-vision models for medical imaging at Glaukos, built data infrastructure and CI/CD at The Commons XR, and shipped tools for neuroscience researchers at Boston College.
